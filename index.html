How the scores and weights were set, and what to be careful about
  • Scale: +5 repeated vocal advocacy; +4 explicit endorsement with a stated reason; +3 says they will support him; +2 defends his record or leadership; +1 mild positive signal; 0 no statement found; -1 declines to commit when asked; -2 calls for new leadership or a leadership debate without ruling him out; -3 withholds support and urges conditions; -4 says it is time for new leadership or backs someone else; -5 flatly says they will not support him.
  • Scores are one reader's judgment of public statements reported in the press. They are not vote predictions: the leadership election is a secret ballot.
  • Weight = 1 + 9 x (years in Congress, House plus Senate, as of January 2027) / 50. The longest-serving member gets 10 and a brand-new senator gets 1.
  • Schumer himself is left out. Mary Peltola (Alaska) and Scott Colom (Mississippi) are left out because their races were uncalled.
  • The four retiring senators and Governor-elect Klobuchar are current senators, so they are included in the main figure, but they are not expected to vote in the leadership election. The group table shows the figure without them.
  • A score of 0 means nothing was found, not that the senator is neutral. Several are close Schumer allies who have simply not been quoted.
